# Tasks: US-003

- [ ] T001 FR-US-003-001 (maps to US-003-ac-1): The system shall provide a notification history screen that is only accessible to an authenticated customer and, when opened by a customer who has prior notifications within the configured retention period, shall display that customer’s notifications that fall within the retention period, showing at minimum for each notification: notification type, notification date/time, and the channel used (for example, SMS, email, push).
- [ ] T002 FR-US-003-002 (maps to US-003-ac-1, ambiguity disclosure): The system shall define and implement a filtering rule for “relevant notifications” such that only notifications associated to the viewing customer and within the configured retention window are included; any further relevance criteria (for example, filtering by account, severity, or product) are not specified in the story and must not be assumed by the implementation without separate requirements.
- [ ] T003 FR-US-003-003 (maps to US-003-ac-2): For notifications where delivery status is recorded, when a customer opens the detail view (or equivalent drill-down) for a specific notification from the notification history, the system shall display the delivery outcome per attempted channel (for example, SMS, email, push) indicating at least whether each attempt was successfully delivered or failed.
- [ ] T004 FR-US-003-004 (maps to US-003-ac-2, ambiguity disclosure): If multiple delivery attempts exist for the same notification and channel (for example, retries), the story does not specify whether each attempt or only the final status must be displayed; the implementation shall document which interpretation it follows and tests shall verify consistency with that documented behavior.
- [ ] T005 FR-US-003-005 (maps to US-003-ac-3): The system shall enforce a 90-day retention window for notification history based on a configurable MongoDB retention setting such that, when a customer opens notification history, notifications older than 90 days (or the configured value, if changed) are not displayed in the history results.
- [ ] T006 FR-US-003-006 (maps to US-003-ac-3, ambiguity disclosure): The story does not specify whether “90-day retention” is calculated from notification creation time, last update time, or delivery time; the implementation shall select one timestamp as the retention basis, document the choice, and ensure that history queries consistently apply that basis in filtering.
- [ ] T007 FR-US-003-007 (maps to US-003-ac-4): When an authenticated customer who has no notifications within the retention period opens the notification history screen, the system shall display an empty-state view with an explanatory message in the customer’s selected language and shall not raise an error message or technical error state to the user.
- [ ] T008 FR-US-003-008 (maps to US-003-ac-5): The notification history list and any associated detail views shall conform to the application’s accessibility baseline and WCAG 2.1 AA so that all notification items, metadata, and delivery statuses are perceivable and operable when a customer uses a screen reader, high-contrast mode, or adjusted text size (for example, through proper semantic markup, focus order, contrast ratios, and text scaling support as defined by the app baseline).
- [ ] T009 FR-US-003-009 (maps to US-003-ac-5, ambiguity disclosure): The specific accessibility techniques (for example, ARIA roles, labels, or keyboard interaction models) are not fully specified in the story; the implementation shall adhere to the platform’s standard accessibility patterns and the app’s documented accessibility baseline, and tests shall verify that all notification history content and controls remain accessible under screen reader, high-contrast, and text scaling scenarios.
- [ ] T010 FR-US-003-010 (maps to US-003-ac-6): When notification history is displayed for a customer whose locale is set to one of the baseline languages (de, fr, it, en), the system shall localize date/time formatting and all user-visible textual content on the notification history and detail views according to that locale (including the empty-state message).
- [ ] T011 FR-US-003-011 (maps to US-003-ac-6, ambiguity disclosure): The story does not specify behavior for locales outside the de/fr/it/en baseline set; the implementation shall not introduce additional localization behaviors for other locales under this story and any such behavior must be covered by separate requirements.
- [ ] T012 FR-US-003-012 (maps to US-003-ac-7): The system shall restrict notification history data such that a customer can only retrieve and view notifications that are associated with their own profile and accounts, enforcing existing privacy and access-control rules so cross-customer or unauthorized account notifications cannot be accessed or displayed.
- [ ] T013 FR-US-003-013 (maps to US-003-ac-7): Storage and retrieval of notification history and its underlying audit records shall rely on existing encryption and access-control mechanisms so that audit records related to notifications remain protected against unauthorized access, in line with applicable privacy and security rules; this story does not introduce new cryptographic schemes beyond what is already defined by the platform.
- [ ] T014 Verify US-003-ac-1: Given an authenticated customer with prior notifications within the retention period, When they open the notification history screen, Then relevant notifications are displayed with at least the type, date/time, and channel used.
- [ ] T015 Verify US-003-ac-2: Given delivery status is recorded for notifications, When the customer views a specific notification in history, Then they can see whether it was successfully delivered or failed for each attempted channel where applicable.
- [ ] T016 Verify US-003-ac-3: Given notifications older than the configured 90-day MongoDB retention window, When the customer opens notification history, Then notifications beyond that retention window are not displayed.
- [ ] T017 Verify US-003-ac-4: Given a customer with no notifications in the retention period, When they open notification history, Then the application displays an appropriate empty-state message in their selected language instead of an error.
- [ ] T018 Verify US-003-ac-5: Given a customer uses a screen reader, high-contrast mode, or adjusted text size, When they navigate the notification history, Then all items and statuses are accessible and readable in accordance with WCAG 2.1 AA and the app’s accessibility baseline.
- [ ] T019 Verify US-003-ac-6: Given the customer’s locale is set to one of the baseline languages (de/fr/it/en), When notification history is displayed, Then date/time and textual content are localized according to that locale.
- [ ] T020 Verify US-003-ac-7: Given applicable privacy and access-control rules, When a customer views notification history, Then they can only see notifications related to their own profile and accounts, and underlying audit records remain protected by encryption and access controls.
